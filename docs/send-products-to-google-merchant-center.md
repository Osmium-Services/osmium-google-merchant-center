**Google Merchant Center** sends your products to Google whenever a product is saved, its status changes or it is removed. Discontinued and unpriced products are removed instead.

## Set it up

1. Open **Google Merchant Center Settings**.
2. Enter your **Merchant ID** and the **Data Source ID**, the primary API data source products are pushed into.
3. Choose the **Content Language**. The feed label is your store's country, from Shop settings.
4. Enter the **Service Account Email** and paste the **Service Account Private Key**. The key is stored on the server and never shown again.
5. Tick **Enabled** and press **Save Settings**.

Use **Test connection** under **Tools** to check it works. The Google side of this is fiddly, so ask your developer if you get stuck.
